BMP: for a production business the PMS is 'the heart and soul of the

| 5:45 pm     | Evening report in 'JSS Reporting' WhatsApp group                                           | Everyone                                                       |

## 6.2 Planning steps

1.  Only fully-kitted jobs enter the plan (Rule 2).

2.  Decide the die-cut queue for the next 1–2 days first; route each job
    Auto or Manual.

3.  Group jobs with similar board or die setup back-to-back to cut
    setting time.

4.  Release to the press only jobs that have a die-cut slot.

5.  Urgent request → Utkarsh decides within 30 min; pushed job named;
    Preeti logs it; that client informed same day.

6.  Plan vs Actual sheet per machine updated at 1 pm and 5 pm (BMP: data
    every 4 hours; delayed data is corrupted data).

## 6.3 Changeover and machine care

- Next die checked, board staged, foil block ready and job card at the
  machine **before** the current job ends (Checklist A5).

- Operator writes setting start/end, sheets run, sheets wasted on the
  job card.

- Weekly preventive check of die-cut, press and finishing machines
  (Saturday), done by operators, signed by Ajay, verified by Anushka.

- Breakdown: Ajay informs Utkarsh + Amit Sir immediately; downtime hours
  recorded.

# 7. IMS — Only for Repeat Boards

BMP Core Process 2. JSS buys most paper job by job, which is right for
job-specific sizes and GSMs. But boards that repeat every month should
never stop a job. **Amit Sir still places every order** — the IMS only
tells him when.

1.  Anushka lists the boards used in at least 3 jobs in the last 3
    months (start with the top 10 — BMP: fill IMS in batches).

2.  For each: average sheets used per day, supplier lead time (days),
    safety factor (1.5 normal; 2 if supplier unreliable).

3.  **Max level = daily use × lead time × safety factor** (calculated in
    the Tracker IMS tab).

4.  Stock at or below one-third of max = **RED** → Anushka sends a paper
    request to Amit Sir the same day ('Red is PAAP').

5.  Stock above max = **PURPLE** → lower the max level. Review max
    levels every 15 days; physical stock count monthly.

# 9. MIS Scores, Delegation Sheet & Executive Meeting

## 9.1 One KRA for everyone

BMP (Week 27): **KRA = all work done as per plan. KPI = % of work NOT
done on time.**

- Planned work for a person in a week = their checklist items due +
  flowchart steps assigned to them + Delegation Sheet tasks.

- **Score = −(planned − done on time) ÷ planned × 100.** 10 planned, 6
  on time = **−40**. 0 is perfect.

- BMP's reason for negative scoring: people grow by focusing on what is
  missing.

- Anushka counts planned vs on-time every Saturday from MIS, checklists
  and the Delegation Sheet; enters them in the Tracker.

## 9.2 Delegation Sheet

Every one-time task from Amit Sir or Utkarsh is written in the
Delegation Sheet (Tracker tab) with a due date — **verbal delegation
does not work** (BMP Week 9). This is also where Utkarsh's small tasks
go instead of being done by him.

## 9.3 Weekly Executive Meeting — every Monday, 30 minutes

1.  Held on Monday no matter what; scores filled before the meeting.

2.  Each person reads last week's MIS score and Delegation score.

3.  Each person declares next week's target score.

4.  **Do not ask why** a score was missed in the meeting — problems go
    to Help Tickets and 1-on-1s.

5.  Company numbers: OTD %, full-kit %, urgent insertions, overdue
    payments (Pradeep).

The existing monthly Monday meeting (90 min) stays as the monthly
review: month's scores, rule changes, next phase.

## 9.4 Appraisals

BMP (Week 29): quarterly appraisal = **80% MIS score + 20% Delegation
Sheet score**; increments yearly, capped at 10–20%. Amit Sir decides
whether to adopt the increment link.

# Appendix — Checklists (5 items each) & Forms

## A1. Job Opening Form (Anushka) — form fields

Client & contact · item/carton (one job no. per carton) · quantity · PO
no. or 'Verbal, confirmed by \_\_\_\_' · board, GSM, size · finishing ·

## A2. Artwork checklist (Puneet)

- Size and dieline match the job

- Text, barcode, mandatory details checked

- Colours specified (CMYK / Pantone)

- Finishing marked (foil, spot UV, emboss)

- Sent to client; date in MIS

## A3. Consumables checklist (Puneet)

- Plates, die, positives, foil block ordered within 24 hrs of approval

- Order and expected dates in MIS

- On arrival: matches job and dieline, no damage

- Received date in MIS

- Delay from vendor raised in PMS meeting

## A4. Full Kit checklist (Preeti — before planning)

- Job number in MIS

- Client-approved artwork

- Plates and die received

- Paper/board in stock and issued

- Foil block / special material ready (if needed)

## A5. Changeover checklist (Ajay — before current die-cut job ends)

- Next die checked (right die, no damage)

- Board counted and staged beside the machine
